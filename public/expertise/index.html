How a project starts

Four steps from your first idea to a clear price.

  1. Step 1

    Initial requirement gathering

    The first meeting, where you present your idea and all initial project aspects are discussed.

  2. Step 2

    Developing a scope

    From what you share, we build a scope showing how the different modules work together to deliver your idea.

  3. Step 3

    Confirming the scope

    A second meeting to review the scope and execution plan together and set every expectation.

  4. Step 4

    Pricing the project

    Once the scope is confirmed, you receive pricing based on the effort needed to execute your idea.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before we talk.

What does the KUWAITNET Project Development Division build?+

Informative websites, catalog web apps, e-commerce and mobile apps (iOS and Android), complex API integrations, SaaS and custom software, and workflow automation with agentic AI.

Do you build with Python and Django?+

Yes. KUWAITNET adopted Python and Django for development in 2007 and invests heavily in the open source community. Python is also the leading language for AI and automation.

How does a project start and how is it delivered?+

A project starts with requirement gathering, a scope and its confirmation, then pricing. Delivery runs through seven phases: workshop, wireframes and designs, SRS (system requirement specifications), development, testing and QA, training and UAT, and go live, with client approval at the key gates.

What support is available after launch?+

Three packages: ODP (ongoing development hours for a clear roadmap and constant changes), yearly maintenance and support for mature projects, and CR (change request) contracts for a single feature.

Which other services does KUWAITNET offer?+

Cloud solutions (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), enterprise solutions (Red Hat, Redis), cyber security (penetration testing, WAF and DDoS, 24/7 SOC), and technology services such as domain registration, hosting, SSL certificates, Google Workspace and Acronis backup.

Where is KUWAITNET based and how do I start?+

KUWAITNET is based in Kuwait City, at Dar Al Awadhi Tower, floors 24 and 25. Our working hours are 8 AM to 5 PM. You can call us on 1807060 or 99747060, or visit us anytime during working hours.

We have an RFP or RFQ. How should we share it?+

Send the requirements to info@kuwaitnet.com or najmuddin@kuwaitnet.com and we take care of the rest.
